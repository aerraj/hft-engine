# Reading and watching

User-provided reading list; bibliographic details and current availability have not been independently verified.

- Sourav Ghosh, *Building Low Latency Applications with C++*: worked trading-system architecture.
- Björn Andrist and Viktor Sehr, *C++ High Performance*: allocation, locality and profiling.
- Larry Harris, *Trading and Exchanges*: market structure and execution mechanics.
- Cartea, Jaimungal and Penalva, *Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading*: inventory-aware market making.
- Carl Cook, CppCon 2017, “When a Microsecond Is an Eternity”.
- David Gross (Optiver), “Trading at Light Speed”.
- NASDAQ TotalView-ITCH 5.0 and OUCH specifications: establish exact wire layouts before expanding protocol support.
- Herb Sutter and Fedor Pikus talks on atomics and lock-free structures: review SPSC memory ordering and concurrency validation.

Suggested sequence: Harris alongside the protocol specifications, Ghosh for integration, Andrist/Sehr for measured optimization, then market-making models and the talks.
